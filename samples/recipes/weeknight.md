# Weeknight

## Lemon Garlic Pasta
Serves 4. 20 minutes.
### Ingredients
- 400g spaghetti
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, zested
### Instructions
Boil the pasta. Emulsify the oil with pasta water. See "Garlic Butter Shrimp" for a variation.
